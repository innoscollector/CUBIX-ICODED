# Module 1: Hardware Architecture & Material Science

## 1. Operating Environment
The CUBIX-ICODED system is classified as an Asymmetric Synchronous Reluctance Machine (SRM) operating under extreme conditions. To prevent atmospheric friction at hyper-velocities and manage thermal loads, the system must operate in:
* **Absolute Vacuum**, OR
* **Inert Plasma Environment** (Argon-Krypton mixture) for advanced EMF stabilization.

## 2. External Housing (The Cube)
The outer containment structure is designed to handle extreme radial stress and contain potential ballistic fragmentation.
* **Material:** Grade 5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V).
* **Geometry & Scale:** Cubical external dimensions of 33 cm.
* **Wall Thickness:** 8 mm minimum.
* **Mechanical Tolerances:** Press-fit assembly is strictly forbidden due to thermal expansion risks under plasma state. A precise **thermal clearance of 0.2 mm to 0.5 mm** is mandatory to prevent structural stress and allow dynamic thermal expansion.

## 3. Internal Static Geometries & Shielding
The system utilizes nested polyhedral structures, each serving a distinct electromagnetic purpose:
* **The Rhombic Triacontahedron:** * *Function:* Primary electromagnetic shielding and EMF Cusp confinement.
  * *Role:* Stabilizes the magnetic flux lines escaping the rotor, preventing chaotic induction in the outer titanium shell.
* **The Pentagonal Bipyramid:**
  * *Function:* Central structural support for the levitating stator core.
  * *Material Constraint:* Must house a stator core made of **Pure Bismuth**.
  * *Role:* Bismuth's extreme diamagnetic properties are utilized for magnetic decoupling, forcing the magnetic flux outward and preventing core saturation.
