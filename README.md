## 1. Concept Overview
CUBIX-ICODED is not a traditional electric motor. It is an advanced electromechanical architecture designed to maximize magnetic flux efficiency and overcome thermodynamic and magnetic locking ("cogging torque"). The system utilizes icosahedral geometry, extreme diamagnetic materials, and plasma-shielding to achieve a highly efficient asymmetric switched reluctance drive.

The core innovation lies in deliberately breaking magnetic symmetry to bypass the traditional conservative nature of permanent magnetic fields, represented by the work equation in a closed loop:
$$ \oint \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = 0 $$
By introducing spatial and structural asymmetry, the system forces a continuous state of dynamic imbalance, translating magnetic potential energy into maximum rotational torque.

## 2. Core Architecture & Physics

### 2.1 The Icosahedral Rotor & The 31.71° Critical Angle
Unlike standard radial motors, the rotor utilizes an Icosahedral geometry. The magnetic flux is specifically channeled at the critical angle of 31.71°. This exact coordinate directs and concentrates the magnetic field lines inward, drastically reducing flux leakage and ensuring maximum interaction with the stator.

### 2.2 Asymmetric Halbach Arrays (5+1 / 1+5)
To prevent the rotor from locking in a state of magnetic equilibrium (dead center), the magnetic nodes on the icosahedral vertices are configured in an asymmetrical Halbach-type array. 
The 5+1 / 1+5 distribution creates a deliberate gradient of magnetic pressure:
*   The imbalance forces the rotor to "fall" from high-pressure zones to low-pressure zones continuously.
*   The vectors are aligned to create a blind spot at the trailing edge (preventing magnetic drag) and double impact force at the leading edge.

### 2.3 The "Spearhead" (Vârf de Lance) Vector Convergence Calculus
A critical, previously unreleased architectural feature is the physical arrangement of the magnets on the outer icosahedral vertices. Each primary exterior vertex is not composed of a single flat magnet, but rather **5 individual magnets converging to form a "Spearhead" (pentagonal pyramid topology)**.
*   **Vector Superposition:** Because 5 equilateral faces meet at every icosahedral vertex, placing a magnetic dipole along each of these 5 converging planes forces their individual magnetic flux vectors ($\mathbf{B}_i$) to intersect at the absolute outer tip.
*   **The Multiplier Effect:** The total magnetic flux density at the spearhead apex is calculated as the vector sum of the 5 individual fields, focused by the convergence angle ($\phi$):
$$ \mathbf{B}_{apex} = \sum_{i=1}^{5} \mathbf{B}_i \cdot \cos(\phi) $$
*   This geometry acts as a macroscopic magnetic lens. It compresses the repulsive/attractive force into a microscopic focal point, allowing the rotor to "pierce" through the stator's resistance fields rather than pushing flatly against them.

### 2.4 Diamagnetic Levitation via Bismuth Bipyramid
The central stator consists of a Pentagonal Bipyramid constructed from 99.99% pure Bismuth. 
*   **Function:** Bismuth is the most naturally diamagnetic element. It repels the permanent magnetic fields of the rotor, creating a frictionless, self-centering axial levitation.
*   **Equatorial Asymmetry:** The bipyramid features 9 vertices at the equator, contrasting with the 10 peri-equatorial points of the Icosahedral rotor. This 9:10 mismatch guarantees that the system never reaches a perfect magnetic lock, maintaining a constant rotational bias.

### 2.5 The Golden Ratio in Switching (3 Motor / 9 Generator)
The outer dodecahedral containment shell features 12 interaction gates, divided asymmetrically:
*   **3 Impulse Gates (Motor):** Arranged in an equilateral triangle, these gates deliver a micro-second electrical pulse from high-discharge capacitors. This minimal energy input overcomes the final resistance at the weakest magnetic point.
*   **9 Harvesting Gates (Generator):** These gates extract energy from the rapidly fluctuating magnetic field generated by the high-speed rotation of the rotor.

### 2.6 Argon-Krypton Plasma Shielding in Vacuum
To combat Lenz's Law (inductive braking), the entire core operates inside a vacuum-sealed Grade 5 Titanium casing, injected with an Argon-Krypton plasma mixture. The plasma acts as an active, ultra-fast diamagnetic shield, absorbing heat and dampening the magnetic signature before the reverse inductive reaction can fully brake the rotor.

## 3. Materials & Fabrication Specifications
For any 1:1 scale prototyping, standard polymers and magnets will suffer catastrophic failure due to shear stress and magnetic forces.
*   **Magnets:** Samarium-Cobalt (SmCo) ONLY. Selected for extreme resistance to demagnetization and thermal stability.
*   **Rotor/Stator Framework:** Must be 3D printed using industrial-grade PEEK or Carbon Fiber reinforced filaments (e.g., utilizing enclosed, high-temp systems like the Creality K2 Plus).
*   **Casing:** Titanium Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) for high tensile strength and non-magnetic properties.

## Overview
CUBIX-ICODED is an open-source experimental electromechanical research platform focused on asymmetric electromagnetic geometry, modular rotor/stator architectures, and high-speed nested polyhedral systems. 

The project combines:
- Icosahedral rotor geometry (vertex-centric magnetic distribution)
- Dodecahedral stator topology (phase-offset harvesting)
- Halbach-inspired 4:1 magnetic star clusters
- Pentagonal Bipyramidal diamagnetic core
- Parametric CAD/CNC construction
- Vacuum/plasma-compatible structural design

CUBIX-ICODED is presented as an evolving engineering research platform intended for CAD prototyping, electromagnetic experimentation, rotational dynamics analysis, flux distribution studies, and collaborative open-source development.

## Development Philosophy
**observe → measure → refine → repeat**

The system is not presented as a finalized technology, but as a continuously evolving experimental architecture. All energetic, electromagnetic, or plasma-related hypotheses remain under active investigation and require independent measurement and validation.

## Core Architecture & Geometric Stack
The system is organized concentrically into 5 functional layers:
1. **External Frame:** Cubic Titanium Grade 5 structure (330 mm, 8 mm wall thickness).
2. **Shielding:** Rhombic Triacontahedron for EMF Cusp confinement and flux stabilization.
3. **Stator:** Dodecahedral system for coil nesting.
4. **Rotor:** Icosahedral matrix for high-speed dynamic flux projection.
5. **Central Core:** Pentagonal Bipyramidal structure utilizing high-purity Bismuth (diamagnetic) for field stabilization without inducing Foucault currents on the central axis.

## Technical Specifications

### Rotor Architecture (Icosahedron)
- **Geometry:** 20 equilateral triangles, 115.34 mm edge length.
- **Assembly Angle:** 20.905° bevel (chamfer) on edges for structural interlocking.
- **Materials:** PEEK-CF / composite experimental variants (3 mm to 8 mm variable thickness).
- **Magnetic Distribution:** 60 SmCo magnets (25x10x5 mm) organized in 12 clusters. Each cluster forms a pentagonal star at the icosahedron's vertices, forcing a 4:1 asymmetric Halbach projection.

### Stator & Phase-Offset Harvesting (Dodecahedron)
- **Ports:** 12 total (3 impulse gates for excitation, 9 harvesting gates).
- **Angular Offset:** The architecture imposes a strict **31.71°** angular offset between the harvesting gates and the primary magnetic vectors.
- **Objective:** Investigating phase displacement behavior, asymmetric field interaction, and reactive drag reduction (Back-EMF management).
- **Topology:** The 9 harvesting ports are grouped into 3 triplets for distributed load balancing and capacitive recovery experimentation.

### Mechanical & Operational Parameters
- **Air Gap:** Target radial clearance of **0.30 mm**.
- **Precession Tolerance:** Experimental induced precession target of **0.02 mm**.
- **Assembly Precision:** Gate positioning tolerance of **±0.005 mm**; angular positioning of **±0.01°**.
- **Operational Environment:** Designed for controlled vacuum environments (10^-6 Torr) with optional inert Argon/Krypton plasma injection to manipulate ionic conductivity.
- **Rotational Target:** Phase 1 prototyping at 2,000 - 3,000 RPM; final structural validation at 25,000 RPM.
