## 1. Material & Nomenclature Upgrades

### Item 4.3: High-Coercivity Permanent Magnets
* **Previous Specification:** SmCo N52, 25×10×5 mm
* **Corrected Specification:** **Samarium-Cobalt Grade SmCo30H** (or XG30)
* **Engineering Rationale:** "N52" is an exclusive classification for Neodymium (NdFeB) magnets, which suffer catastrophic thermal degradation above 80°C–120°C. Since the vacuum gap environment ($10^{-6}\text{ Torr}$) completely lacks convective cooling, eddy currents and plasma radiative heat will rapidly push core temperatures past the NdFeB threshold. **SmCo30H** maintains mechanical and magnetic stability up to 300°C, with a high remanence ($B_r \approx 1.08\text{ T}$).
* **Polarity Vector Rule (Halbach Cluster Orientation):** To properly assemble the 5-element asymmetric Spearhead cluster on each vertex, the magnetic axes must follow a mandatory spatial rotation. Magnet 3 (Apex/Center) must be oriented radially **NORD → Exterior** for the *5+1* configuration, and **SUD → Exterior** for the *1+5* configuration, while the 4 surrounding planar magnets must point their N-poles towards the active compression face to focus the total flux vector ($\mathbf{B}_{apex}$).

### Item 4.5: Rotor Shell Alignment & Cluster Retention
* **Previous Specification:** Ø3 mm × 10 mm stainless 316L dowel pins
* **Corrected Specification:** **Ø6 mm × 12 mm High-Tensile Titanium (Grade 5) or High-Strength Alloy Steel Pins (Min. Grade 12.9)**
* **Engineering Rationale:** Finite Element Analysis (FEA) indicates that at the 25,000 RPM validation threshold, the centrifugal load acting on each 5-magnet cluster reaches **39.5 kN**. Under these conditions, a standard Ø3 mm or M3 dowel pin experiences a shear stress exceeding **1,117 MPa**, far surpassing the 400 MPa shear limit of standard steel, resulting in immediate catastrophic mechanical failure. Upgrading to high-tensile Ø6 mm/Ø8 mm retention hardware keeps structural shear stress well within safe limits (<280 MPa).

### Item 2.0: EMF Shielding Layer (Rhombic Triacontahedron)
* **Previous Specification:** Mu-metal 0.5 mm sheet (pre-annealed)
* **Alternative Specification:** **Metglas 2705M (Cobalt-based Amorphous Metal Ribbon) or Scheduled AC-DeGaussing Protocol**
* **Engineering Rationale:** Standard pre-annealed Mu-metal is highly effective for static or low-frequency magnetic shielding. However, exposure to the severe, high-frequency rotating magnetic fields (5 kHz fundamental at 25k RPM) generated by the SmCo30H Halbach clusters will rapidly induce permanent magnetic saturation in the Mu-metal, rendering its high permeability useless. **Metglas 2705M** offers near-zero magnetic hysteresis losses in high-frequency dynamic fields and supreme resistance to permanent magnetization. If Mu-metal is used, an automated electronic degaussing circuit must be implemented between active runs.
