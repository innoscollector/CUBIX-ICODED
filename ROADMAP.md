# CUBIX-ICODED: Project Roadmap & Testing Phases

This roadmap outlines the physical construction, mechanical validation, and electromagnetic testing phases for the full-scale (33 cm) CUBIX-ICODED prototype. The progression is strictly sequential to ensure structural integrity and operational safety.

## Phase 1: Procurement & Rapid Prototyping (Pre-Assembly)
* [ ] **Rotor Fabrication:** 3D printing the regular icosahedron core using high-performance PEEK-CF. Strict adherence to the `< 0.1 mm` alignment tolerance for mounting slots.
* [ ] **Housing Machining:** CNC machining of the Grade 5 Titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) external cubical containment (8 mm wall thickness).
* [ ] **Hardware Sourcing:** Procurement of custom high-tensile pins (Ø6 mm × 12 mm), Pure Bismuth for the diamagnetic core, and 60 Samarium-Cobalt (SmCo) magnets.

## Phase 2: Static Assembly & Structural Validation
* [ ] **Halbach Array Integration:** Securing the pentagram-star magnet clusters at the 12 vertices. *Caution: Managing the 200–500 N repulsive forces per magnet during assembly.*
* [ ] **Air-Gap Calibration:** Mechanical verification of the precise **0.50 mm** clearance between the PEEK-CF rotor and the stator.
* [ ] **Thermal Clearance Check:** Verifying the 0.2–0.5 mm thermal gap in the titanium housing to prevent press-fit structural stress.
* [ ] **Dynamic Balancing:** Multi-plane static and dynamic balancing of the assembled rotor. Must achieve a residual unbalance of **< 0.01 g**.

## Phase 3: Low-Velocity & Electromagnetic Testing
* [ ] **MHD Fluid Integration (Galinstan):** Injecting and sealing the Galinstan liquid metal modules for magnetohydrodynamic vortex visualization and active gyroscopic stabilization.
* [ ] **Low-RPM Induction Tests:** Initiating low-speed rotation to monitor the sequential engagement of the 12 stator gates.
* [ ] **Phase Shift Validation:** Oscilloscope measurement of the coil induction to verify the temporal delay caused by the **31.71° geometric offset**, confirming the tangential slip of Lenz's Law counter-torque.

## Phase 4: High-Velocity Stress Tests (Full-Scale)
* [ ] **Firmware Lock Activation:** Ensuring the ICODED safety limit is strictly locked at **25,000 RPM** ($\omega = 2618 \text{ rad/s}$).
* [ ] **Kinetic Spin-Up:** Gradual acceleration to peak velocity inside a controlled test bunker.
* [ ] **Deformation Monitoring:** Laser-measuring the rotor to confirm the calculated **0.316 mm** elastic radial expansion under the 39.5 kN centrifugal load.
* [ ] **Sustained Operation:** Monitoring thermal loads, Bismuth core magnetic deflection efficiency, and overall EMF Cusp confinement stability.
