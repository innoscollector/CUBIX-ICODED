## 1. Stator Topology & Gate Configuration
The stationary boundary of the system is engineered to interface directly with the multi-polar spherical flux generated by the icosahedral rotor.
* **Stator Geometry:** Regular Dodecahedron (12 pentagonal faces).
* **Induction Gates:** The architecture integrates 12 electromagnetic gates distributed symmetrically across the faces:

* **Eddy Current Mitigation:** Gate cores and coil assemblies utilize specific lamination or dielectric shielding to minimize parasitic thermal losses.

## 2. Central Diamagnetic Core (Magnetic Decoupling)
At the exact geometric center of the stator, suspended by the pentagonal bipyramid support structure, lies the core stabilization matrix.
* **Material:** Pure Bismuth (Bi).
* **Engineering Function:** Bismuth possesses the highest stable diamagnetic susceptibility of any element. Instead of absorbing or conducting magnetic fields, it actively repels them. 
* **Core Saturation Prevention:** The bismuth core acts as an absolute magnetic insulator. It forces the flux lines escaping the Samarium-Cobalt clusters to remain concentrated within the 0.50 mm air-gap and the active stator gates, preventing central core saturation and ensuring high flux density (0.8–1.4 Tesla).

## 3. The 31.71° Critical Angle & Inductive Delay Mechanics
The geometric interplay between the 12 vertices of the rotor and the 12 faces of the stator is calculated to shift electromagnetic counter-forces out of phase.
* **Mathematical Derivation:** The critical offset angle of **31.71°** is derived from the dual polyhedral relationship between the icosahedron and the dodecahedron, governed by the Golden Ratio ($\phi = 1.618$):
  $$\theta \approx \arccos\left(\frac{1}{\phi^2}\right) \approx 31.71^\circ$$
* **Temporal Asymmetry:** This spatial offset ensures that magnetic poles never align head-on or simultaneously with the stator gates. The inductive response is governed by the coil time constant:
  $$\tau = \frac{L}{R}$$
* **Tangential Slip Effect:** In standard synchronous machines, pole alignment causes Lenz's Law counter-torque to hit the rotational axis perpendicularly. In CUBIX-ICODED, the cascading engagement of the pentagram-star magnet arrays delays the induced magnetic reaction. The opposing forces do not collide frontally; instead, they "slip" tangentially, allowing the rotor's kinetic inertia to pass through peak induction points without major parasitic deceleration.
