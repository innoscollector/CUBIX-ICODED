The magnetic foundation of the CUBIX-ICODED rotor relies on the concentration of flux through an extreme multi-polar Halbach configuration. The magnetic flux density ($B$) generated by the Samarium-Cobalt (SmCo) clusters is approximated by:

$$B \approx B_r(1-e^{-kt})$$

* $B_r$: Remanence of the SmCo magnets (approx. 1.1 T).
* $k$: Magnet thickness coefficient.
* $t$: Geometric spatial factor.
* **Localized Field Target:** The pentagram-star clusters are designed to compress the flux lines, generating localized EMF cusps peaking between **0.8 and 1.4 Tesla** at the 0.50 mm air-gap boundary.

## 2. Lorentz Force Vectors & Tangential Slip
To bypass the standard Lenz's Law counter-torque, the system relies on the **31.71° temporal phase shift**. The standard Lorentz force equation ($\mathbf{F} = q(\mathbf{E} + \mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})$) is manipulated through geometric delay.

Because the induction response is delayed by the stator coil's time constant ($\tau = L/R$), the opposing magnetic field is generated *after* the primary rotor pole has passed top dead center (TDC). The resulting cross product ($\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}$) directs the opposing force tangentially rather than axially, turning a head-on collision into a magnetic "slip".

## 3. Kinetic & Centrifugal Stress Equations
The physical survival of the system relies on containing the massive kinetic energy generated at operational speeds. For the full-scale 33 cm model operating at **25,000 RPM**, the angular velocity ($\omega$) reaches **2618 rad/s**.

* **Centrifugal Acceleration ($a$):**
  $$a = r\omega^2 \approx 1.13 \times 10^6 \, \text{m/s}^2 \, (\approx 115,000 \, g)$$
* **Tangential Stress ($\sigma$):**
  $$\sigma = \rho r^2 \omega^2$$
  The resulting internal shear forces closely approach the **1.2 GPa** tensile yield strength of the Grade 5 Titanium housing. This mathematical threshold is the absolute reason why the firmware hard-locks the maximum speed at 25,000 RPM to prevent ballistic fragmentation.

## 4. FEA/FEM Simulation Roadmap
Validation of the CUBIX-ICODED architecture cannot be performed using standard static magnetic solvers. Future computational simulations require advanced multi-physics environments (e.g., COMSOL Multiphysics, ANSYS Maxwell, or CST Studio).
